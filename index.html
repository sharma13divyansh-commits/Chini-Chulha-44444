About Us

Crafting Flavors That Bring People Together

Chini Chulha is a food destination where delicious flavors meet a welcoming dining experience. Established in August 2026, we bring together a variety of food options, refreshing beverages, and satisfying meal combinations.

Whether you are dropping in for a quick afternoon snack, savoring an evening kulhad chai, enjoying a hot bubbling pizza with loved ones, or treating yourself to our value combo meals, Chini Chulha offers a comfortable, friendly space designed for great conversations and even better food.
